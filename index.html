Garden Mechanics & Rules Guide

1. Core Energy & Water Bucket System

  • Energy Cost: Performing tool actions (tilling, watering, planting, fertilizing, harvesting, or clearing) costs 5 Energy per tile action.
  • Energy Reset: Clicking End Day completely restores your energy back to maximum (100).
  • Watering Bucket: Watering soil or crops consumes 1L of water. Select the Watering Can and click the Infinite Well to refill your bucket (costs 10 Energy).

2. Tool Arsenal & Area-of-Effect (AOE) Upgrades

  • Shovel: Clears/reverts a soil tile and removes planted or wilted crops.
  • Hoe: Tills unplowed soil plots into farmable land.
  • Watering Can: Waters tilled soil or growing crops (required daily on Sunny days).
  • Scythe: Harvests fully matured crops into your inventory hotbar.
  • Seeder: Plants your active selected hotbar seed onto tilled soil.
  • Plant Defense Fertilizer: Applies a protective shield to an unshielded active crop to mitigate overnight disaster damage.
  • AOE Range Unlocks: Leveling up Wumbus expands your tool impact range from 1x1 up to 2x2, 3x3, and 4x4 multi-tile areas!

3. Seasons, Weather Conditions, & Wilting Rules

  • Seasonal Queue: Time rotates through Spring, Summer, Fall (Autumn), and Winter every 30 in-game days.
  • Sunny Weather: Soil dries out overnight. Crops must be watered daily with your Watering Can, or they will wilt overnight.
  • Rainy Weather: Rain automatically waters all tilled soil tiles at the start of the day.
  • Seasonal Wilting: Crops left planted when the season transitions to an incompatible season will immediately wilt.

4. Weather Channel Forecast Radar & Overnight Disasters

  • Tomorrow's Forecast Radar: Click the Weather Channel TV set in the top header to query the 30-day Binary Search Tree (BST) for tomorrow's exact weather and environmental hazards in $O(\log N)$ time.
  • TV Alert Dot: The LED indicator dot on the TV set glows Green when tomorrow is clear and flashes Red when a hazard is detected for tomorrow.
  • Overnight Disasters: Advancing the day has a chance to trigger overnight environmental hazards (Meteorite Strike, Alien Escape, Green Rain Windstorm, or Earthquake).
  • Fertilizer Protection: Applying Plant Defense Fertilizer shields active crops against overnight disaster damage so they survive strike events!

5. Wumbus Quests, EXP Progression, & Mystery Shop

  • Hierarchical Quest Tree: Wumbus requests specific quantities of seasonal crops. Select the crop in your hotbar and click "Give" in the Quests tab to submit it.
  • Leveling & Rewards: Submitting quest crops grants EXP and Gold. Leveling up unlocks higher tool AOE tiers, bonus coins, and expanded water bucket capacity!
  • Tiered Mystery Seed Bags:
    • Tier 1 Bag (15 Coins): Common 70% | Uncommon 25% | Rare 5%
    • Tier 2 Bag (30 Coins): Common 40% | Uncommon 45% | Rare 15%
    • Tier 3 Bag (50 Coins): Common 20% | Uncommon 50% | Rare 30%
  • Selling Crops: Select surplus harvested crops in your inventory hotbar and navigate to the Sell Tab to sell them for coins.
  • Real-Time Execution Trace: The top right panel dynamically traces every executed Python function, module filename, and underlying Data Structure in real-time!
